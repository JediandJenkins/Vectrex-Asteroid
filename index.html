PILOT BRIEFING

How to Play

Your objective

Pilot your spaceship through dangerous minefields. Destroy every mine to advance to the next wave. Survive as long as possible and earn the highest score!

Larger mines split into smaller enemies when destroyed. Clear every fragment to finish the wave.

Computer controls

Pause / Resume

These are your current bindings. Customize either key for each action in Settings.

Use the fullscreen button or an unassigned F key to expand the view. Escape exits fullscreen first; otherwise your configured pause keys apply.

Mobile controls

  • Joystick Left — Rotate Left.
  • Joystick Right — Rotate Right.
  • Joystick Up — Thrust.
  • Joystick Diagonally Up — Rotate and Thrust.
  • FIRE Button — Shoot projectiles. Hold for continuous fire.

Steer and fire with two fingers at the same time. A gentle sideways push rotates slowly; push farther to turn faster.

Your ship keeps its momentum after you release thrust. Landscape gives you a wider field.

Choose your mode

CLASSIC
Endless waves with increasing difficulty. Survive and achieve the highest score. Start with 3 ships; earn another every 4 completed waves.
CHALLENGE
Complete 13 increasingly difficult minefields to win the game. Start with 3 ships; earn bonus ships after waves 4 and 8. Every large mine splits into 3 medium mines; each medium splits into 2 small mines.
IMPOSSIBLE
One life, extremely dangerous enemies, and rapidly increasing difficulty. Survive as long as possible. No extra lives.

Flight tips

  • Keep moving to avoid enemy collisions.
  • Rotate while thrusting to change direction.
  • Large mines split into smaller ones.
  • Use momentum to your advantage.
  • Be careful when surrounded by enemies.
